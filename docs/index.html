The mission

Hundun Robotics exists for one purpose: to construct a fully autonomous generation vessel capable of sustaining ten thousand human beings across interstellar distances.

The ship will not be assembled by human hands in orbit. It will be grown by successive generations of machines — each generation building the next with greater precision, until the habitat itself becomes a living industrial ecology.

Maintenance, expansion, and repair will remain the domain of robots. Humans will inhabit. Machines will sustain.

Recursive manufacturing

Our core technology is not a single robot. It is a closed industrial loop: extraction, refining, fabrication, assembly, and replication — all performed by machines that can produce improved versions of themselves.

  • Seed units Compact, high-capability founders delivered from Earth. They bootstrap the first factory.
  • Generation cascades Each generation increases in number, specialization, and manufacturing resolution.
  • Autonomous maintenance Damage, wear, and expansion are handled by the swarm without human presence on site.
  • Human-safe zones Once the habitat is sealed, the industrial layers remain isolated. People live; machines work.

Habitat strata

Layered living districts with artificial gravity sections, agriculture rings, and radiation-buffered cores. Designed as a complete ecology, not a temporary capsule.

Industrial exterior

The outer shell remains a working factory. Mining, refining, and replication continue for decades after the first humans arrive — expanding and repairing without risk to the population.

Closed-loop life

Air, water, biomass, and energy are managed by dedicated robotic systems. Human oversight is possible; human labor is not required.

04

The path

Phase 0

Earth factories

Develop and prove recursive manufacturing on Earth. Seed units, replication protocols, quality gates.

Phase 1

Orbital bootstrap

Deploy seed factories in cislunar space. Begin autonomous expansion using local and delivered materials.

Phase 2

Ship growth

The vessel takes form. Habitat modules, shielding, propulsion architecture — all machine-assembled.

Phase 3

Human embarkation

Ten thousand lives enter a ship that has never required a human welder. The journey begins.
